About Moana

Moana is a brave young girl who lives on the island of Motunui. She is chosen by the ocean to restore the heart of Te Fiti. Along her journey, she meets the demigod Maui and discovers her courage, identity, and love for her people.

Why I Like Moana

Moana teaches us to be brave, follow our dreams, and never give up even when the journey becomes difficult.
